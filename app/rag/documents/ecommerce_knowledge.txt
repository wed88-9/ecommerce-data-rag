ORDER POLICY
Orders with quantity greater than zero are considered valid.
Orders with a quantity of zero or a negative quantity are rejected during ingestion.
Each order must have a valid order ID and customer ID.

SHIPPING POLICY
Standard shipping normally takes between 3 and 5 business days.
Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation.
Customers receive order status information after the order has been processed.

CANCELLATION POLICY
Pending orders can be cancelled before shipment.
Completed or shipped orders cannot be cancelled directly and must follow the return policy.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Customers should provide their order ID when asking about a specific order.
Product questions can include price, category, stock status, warranty, and return policy.
